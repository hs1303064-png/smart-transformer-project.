IoT-based monitoring and protection of a three-phase distribution transformer, built on an ESP32.

Introduction

Distribution transformers step the grid voltage down for consumers. Overloading, abnormal voltage and high temperature can damage them, and checking them by hand only gives occasional readings.

This project builds a low-voltage demonstration of continuous monitoring. Three separate 230 V / 12 V, 3 A single-phase transformers represent the R, Y and B units. Sensors feed an ESP32, which displays the values on an LCD, sends them to the Blynk app, and switches off the affected unit when its current or temperature crosses the set limit.

How it works

01
Sense: ZMPT101B modules read voltage and ACS712 modules read current of each unit. A DS18B20 sensor on each transformer reads temperature, and all three share one data pin.
02
Process: The ESP32 converts the sensor signals into voltage, current and temperature values after calibration.
03
Compare: Current is checked against 3.0 A and temperature against 70 °C for every unit separately.
04
Act: If a limit is crossed, the relay of that unit cuts it off, its own cooling fan turns on, and a common buzzer sounds. Other units keep running.
05
Display: Readings and status show on the 20×4 I2C LCD and are sent to the Blynk mobile dashboard, which also gives overload notifications.
